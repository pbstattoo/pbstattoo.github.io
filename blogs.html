From Stencil to Skin: What happens during your session?

1. Sizing & Stencil Placement

We use 100% sterile, single-use cartridge needles and medical-grade barriers.

3. Outlining & Shading

We start with linework, then move to shading like black and grey washes or stippling.

4. Wrap

Once finished, the tattoo gets wiped, wrapped, and we discuss aftercare.

Placement & Pain 3 Min Read
The ultimate guide to tattoo placement and pain zones.
↓

The "Easy" Zones

Outer forearms, thighs, calves. Thicker skin makes them perfect for your first tattoo.

The "Spicy" Zones

Ribs, sternum, hands, and feet. Thin skin over bone means high pain.
