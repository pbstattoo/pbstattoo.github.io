Healing Guide.

Your tattoo is an investment. Proper aftercare ensures the lines stay crisp, the shading stays smooth, and the ink lasts a lifetime.

Phase 01 / The First 24 Hours

Leave the wrap on, then wash gently.

Keep the initial bandage or cling film on for 2 to 4 hours (or as instructed by your artist). Once removed, wash the tattoo immediately using warm water and a fragrance-free, antibacterial liquid soap. Use only your clean fingertips—no cloths or sponges. Pat dry gently with a clean paper towel.

Phase 02 / Days 2 to 3

Keep it clean and dry.

Wash the tattoo 2-3 times a day. During this phase, the tattoo may feel like a sunburn and leak small amounts of ink and plasma. This is completely normal. Allow the tattoo to dry fully after washing.

Phase 03 / Days 4 to 14

Moisturize sparingly.

Once the tattoo starts feeling tight or begins to peel/flake, apply a very thin layer of fragrance-free lotion or tattoo-specific ointment 1-2 times a day. Do not over-moisturize; the skin needs to breathe to heal.

Crucial Rules / What To Avoid

Protect your fresh ink.

  • Do not scratch or pick: Let the scabs and flakes fall off naturally. Picking will pull ink out of the skin and cause patchiness.
  • No swimming or soaking: Avoid pools, hot tubs, the ocean, and long baths for at least 3 weeks. Quick showers only.
  • Avoid direct sunlight: Keep the tattoo out of the sun while it heals. Once fully healed, always apply high-SPF sunscreen to prevent fading.
  • Skip heavy workouts: Avoid intense sweating and tight, abrasive clothing over the tattooed area for the first week.
